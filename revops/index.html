RevOps stack in Fibery: one system for CRM, deals, and reporting

Result: 6 reports consolidated into 1 dashboard, saving 4 hours a week, and manual workload cut by 40% through automation.

Problem

Building a B2B motion from scratch meant deals, contacts, and call notes lived in different places, and reporting took manual work every week.

What I built

  1. Fibery RevOps stack: CRM, transaction management, and contact intelligence in one workspace, backed by SOPs and standardized workflows.
  2. One dashboard replacing 6 reports, with real-time revenue visibility.
  3. Automations in Claude Code and Zapier across sales, marketing, and operations.
  4. Conversation intelligence flow: syncs call records into the CRM, creates a post-call email and follow-up tasks in Fibery, and sends weekly Slack reports on pipeline insights and coaching opportunities.

A key decision

Nothing broke, but this was a choice. When Pinpointe moved to B2B, I didn't build a new CRM. I added Accounts and new views for Contacts to the existing one.
