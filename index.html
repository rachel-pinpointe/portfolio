Systems

01

Outbound GTM system

An automated outbound engine that runs from signal to sequence. It tracks NYC hiring and funding signals, qualifies and enriches contacts, sequences outreach. 4% reply rate and $450K of the $580K direct pipeline. Code: signal-driven-outbound and hypersignal-gtm.

02

NYC listings platform

Parses off-market listings from emails, PDFs, docs, and spreadsheets into one searchable database. Shipped in one month. 1,000+ listings, 6 agents, 3 to 5 hours saved per client search.

03

RevOps stack in Fibery

CRM, transaction management, and contact intelligence in one workspace, backed by SOPs and standardized workflows. 6 reports became 1 real-time dashboard, saving 4 hours a week, and automations cut manual workload by 40%.
